Use and limitations

Fundamental Research Backups · Updated October 26, 2026

This application is maintained for its owner's private use. It is not a public subscription service and does not place trades or manage a brokerage account.

A backup is treated as verified only after the application checks the uploaded files, downloads their exact contents and successfully tests restoration. A verified receipt protects its recorded checkpoint; it does not establish that later changes are backed up.

Operation depends on the research server, network, available storage, Google authorization and the availability of the connected services. No guarantee of uninterrupted operation or permanent authorization is made. Failed operations and recovery gaps require attention.

This website provides information about the backup application, not investment advice or a promise of investment returns.
