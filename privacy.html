Privacy notice

Fundamental Research Backups · Updated October 26, 2026

Purpose and access

This is a personal application for its owner's use. With the owner's authorization, it creates research backup files in Google Drive and downloads them to verify their contents and test restoration. It requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, covering files created by the application or specifically granted to it, rather than unrestricted Drive access.

Information handled

Backup contents include research sources and reports, original project instructions and decisions, software needed for recovery, and operational history. File names, identifiers, sizes, hashes and sharing metadata are used to verify the backup. Google authorization tokens are stored privately on the owner's research server and are excluded from research archives and reports.

Storage and sharing

Recovery files are retained on the owner's research server and in the owner's Google Drive. Backups are intended to be private; the worker checks ownership and sharing metadata before acknowledging a verified backup. The application does not sell this information, publish research archives, or use Drive contents for advertising. Google and the server provider process information as necessary to provide their respective services. This notice does not describe separate research-model processing.

Retention and control

Earlier backups are retained to preserve recovery history. The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account third-party connection settings and can manage files directly in Drive. Revoking access does not delete previously stored copies. Deleting recovery files can prevent restoration; deletion is a separate owner decision.

This website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics code or advertising. The website host may process routine request information under its own privacy policy. The application owner is its only intended user and manages any privacy questions directly through the accounts and services they control.
